Gobble Dash — a turkey escape runner: outrun the farmer 1500m to the forest before Thanksgiving dinner. Jump the fences and hay bales, slide under the low branches, dodge the oven and the falling carving knives, grab corn to win back room from the farmer, and catch a golden feather to smash straight through. The day turns to dusk and then night as you run; reach the forest and you escape.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Play with keyboard or mouse: SPACE / click to jump and start, ↓ to slide, SHIFT to dash, P to pause, M to mute. No build step, no network — the game is plain static files under `game/`, all art and sound drawn and synthesized in code.
